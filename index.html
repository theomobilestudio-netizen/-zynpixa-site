Features

Everything you need to fix photo dates

Simple photo tools built around a single idea: do one job well, keep your originals safe, never upload anything.

✎

Change capture date

Pick one or many photos. Choose Now or a custom date and time. One tap on Apply writes the new EXIF capture date to every photo in the batch.

ℹ

Inspect EXIF metadata

Capture / digitized dates, GPS presence (never coordinates), dimensions, orientation, camera make and model, file name, MIME type, size.

✓

Save as New (default)

Corrected copies go to Pictures/Zynpixa. Your originals stay unchanged. If something fails, nothing was written.

↻

Update Original

Where Android allows it, rewrite the photo in place — only after an explicit confirmation. Clearly disabled when the system cannot grant write access.

⚡

Batch processing

Progress circle, per-item status (Saved / Failed / Skipped), retry failed items, open or share saved copies without leaving the app.

⌂

Works offline

Picking, metadata, editing and saving never need a network. The only network use is the optional rewarded ad.

How to use

Four steps from pick to saved

No account, no sign-in, no setup. Open the app and go.

1

Pick photos

Tap Select Photos on Home. The Android system photo picker opens. Choose one or many photos — Zynpixa only sees what you pick.

💡 Prefer View EXIF on Home to inspect a single photo without starting an edit.
2

Review the selection

Zynpixa shows the chosen photos with file names. Remove any one, add more through the picker, or Clear all to start over.

Tap any thumbnail to open the EXIF viewer for that photo.

3

Choose a new date

In the Date Editor, pick Now (resolved when you tap Apply) or Choose Date & Time (opens the native date and time pickers).

The time zone shown is your device zone. Save mode defaults to Save as New; Update Original is shown when supported, with a clear warning.

💡 Save as New means your originals stay unchanged. Copies are written to Pictures/Zynpixa.
4

Apply and review results

Tap Apply to N photos. A progress hero shows the running count. The results screen lists every photo with Saved / Failed / Skipped status.

Open or share any saved copy, Retry failed items, or tap View saved photos to jump to the output folder.

Edit credits

Three free edits per day. No subscription.

Zynpixa is free. Processing a batch consumes one credit per successful photo. Failed or skipped items consume nothing.

3

Free daily quota

Three successful edits per local day. The counter resets at local midnight.

+10

No forced ads

Honest limits

What Zynpixa can and cannot promise

No fraud-resistance guarantees, no "works everywhere" claims. These are the things the app itself tells you.

Known limitations

  • JPEG only. Other formats can be inspected but are never converted. Non-JPEG items are reported as skipped and consume zero credits.
  • Picker redacts GPS. Android hides GPS values for photos shared through the photo picker, so a saved copy can only keep the location data the app is given.
  • Gallery sorting is not guaranteed. Saving a copy adds a new gallery item with its own date fields; some gallery apps sort by a different date and may not reflect the edit.
  • Update Original has no atomic rollback. Best-effort backup/restore only. It is unavailable for photos chosen through the system picker — Save as New is used there.
  • The daily quota is a local control, not fraud protection. Changing the device clock, reinstalling the app or clearing app data bypasses it.
  • Cloud-backed photos. Android cannot guarantee every cloud-backed URI can be read or saved; failed items show an honest error.

Your photos stay on your device.

Zynpixa processes everything locally. No account, no cloud sync, no uploads. Ads come from Google AdMob with the consent flow Android requires.
