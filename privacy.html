Privacy Policy

What Zynpixa does — and does not — do with your information.

Effective / last updated: 2026-10-11
App: Zynpixa – Photo Tools
Publisher: Theo Duong
Contact: See the contact details listed on the app's Google Play Store page.

This policy explains what the Zynpixa Android app does with your information. It is not legal advice.

1. Photos you select

  • Zynpixa asks you to choose photos through the Android system photo picker. The app only receives the photos you explicitly pick; it has no access to your photo library beyond that selection and declares no storage permissions.
  • Your photos are read and edited entirely on your device. Photo contents and metadata are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared by the app. The app has no backend, no account system, and no cloud sync.
  • Saved copies are written to Pictures/Zynpixa on your device. Update Original (where available) rewrites the photo you consented to edit, in place, on your device.

2. Advertising

Zynpixa shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as:

  • device information (including device/account identifiers and the advertising ID),
  • ad interaction and app usage events relevant to ad delivery,
  • diagnostics such as SDK performance and crash-adjacent signals.

Google processes this data under its own policies; see Google's Privacy & Terms and the AdMob partner policy. Where consent is required (for example in the EEA/UK), Zynpixa shows a consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later from Settings → Privacy options. Ads are requested only after the SDK reports that consent allows it.

Zynpixa itself does not collect or transmit your photos, your edited copies, or their metadata to anyone.

3. What the app stores on your device

  • A local edit quota (free edits per day and earned bonus credits).
  • App settings, including the privacy-preserve toggle, dynamic-color choice, and consent state cached by the UMP SDK.
  • Pending/edited image files while a save is in progress, and your saved copies in Pictures/Zynpixa.

Uninstalling Zynpixa or clearing its app data removes the app's local data (your saved photo copies in Pictures/Zynpixa are normal gallery items and are not removed automatically).

4. Retention

  • Photo data: remains only on your device, for as long as you keep the files.
  • Local app data: kept on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Ad-related data: retained by Google according to Google's policies; Zynpixa does not store it.

5. Your choices

  • Consent / privacy options: Settings → Privacy options (shown where consent is required) lets you change your advertising consent choice; declining does not block editing features.
  • No ads: if you prefer not to watch rewarded ads, the free daily quota still applies and the app remains usable.
  • Permissions: the app's own manifest declares only INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and the ad ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID). The ads SDK adds its own ad-services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), FOREGROUND_SERVICE and WAKE_LOCK through Android's manifest merge; AndroidX contributes an app-scoped dynamic-receiver permission used only by the app itself. The app requests no storage or location permissions.
  • Contact: for privacy questions or deletion requests concerning data you believe we hold, use the contact details listed on the app's Google Play Store page. The app itself holds no server-side data.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app listing notes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

7. Children

Zynpixa is not designed for children. The app does not knowingly collect data from users under the age applicable to your jurisdiction.
